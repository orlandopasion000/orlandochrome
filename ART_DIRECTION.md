# Chrome Synapse — Persistent Visual & Art Direction Specification

> **Permanent User Mandate**: All game icons, powered-up good relics, powered-up bad hazards, characters, and weapon hardware **MUST** match photorealistic AAA **Baroque Fine-Jewelry** (Good) and **Dark-Fantasy / Tactical Obsidian Sci-Fi** (Bad) craftsmanship.
> **NEVER** generate cartoonish, flat-vector, clip-art, low-poly, or plastic Three.js primitive node artwork.

## 1. Realm Visual Identity Pillars (Dynamo · Helix · Meteor Must Always Differ)
- **Chrome Dynamo (`dynamo`)**:
  - **Good Relics**: 24K Royal Antique Gold filigree, Deep Royal Sapphire & Amethyst faceted gemstones, Faraday cyan-gold lightning accents.
  - **Bad Hazards**: Blackened titanium armor plating, electric violet-crimson plasma cores (red-glass EMP lens preserved glowing crimson).
- **Chrome Helix (`helix`)**:
  - **Good Relics**: Brushed Platinum-Silver filigree, Iridescent Aquamarine/Cyan & Orchid-Magenta faceted prism gemstones.
  - **Bad Hazards**: Dark obsidian-silver hardware, neon-magenta & violet-crimson rift crystals (red-glass EMP lens preserved glowing ruby).
- **Chrome Meteor (`meteor`)**:
  - **Good Relics**: Forged Imperial Bronze-Gold scrollwork, Solar-Amber Citrine, Sunburst Gold & Warm Ruby faceted gemstones.
  - **Bad Hazards**: Charred volcanic basalt & scorched bronze iron, incandescent magma orange-scarlet cores.
